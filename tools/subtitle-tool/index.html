What was done in this example?

Renumbered subtitle cues in sequence, trimmed unnecessary whitespace, and shifted the provided timestamps forward by 1.5 seconds. This is a demonstration with known timing, not automatic transcription or full speech synchronization.

For custom projects, we can examine unusual subtitle formatting and correct problems according to the supplied requirements. Manual synchronization against video or speech needs a separate quote.
